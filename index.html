About Our Campaign

"Less Screen, More Life" is a movement to inspire people to spend less time on devices and more time enjoying real life. We believe that reducing screen time can help you feel happier, healthier, and more connected to the world around you.

Join us to discover new hobbies, strengthen relationships, and improve your well-being. Every moment away from the screen is a step towards a better you!

Why Less Screen, More Life?

Boost Your Mental Health

Reducing screen time helps lower stress, anxiety, and improves your mood.

Get Active Outdoors

More time away from screens means more time for exercise and outdoor fun.

Connect With Loved Ones

Enjoy real moments with friends and family, not just virtual ones.
